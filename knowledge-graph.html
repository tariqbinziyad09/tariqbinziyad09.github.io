PROJECT OVERVIEW

What does this project do?

The project was designed to provide a concept-based search experience across three religious sources: Quran, Hadith and Bible.

Instead of depending only on exact keyword matching, the system is designed to understand related concepts and return relevant passages, references and relationships.

Users can search concepts such as "God", "Messenger" or other topics and explore related information through an interactive knowledge graph.

THE PROBLEM

Why did I build it?

Traditional keyword-based search can struggle when different words represent the same or related ideas. The goal of this project was to provide a more intelligent way to explore religious texts by connecting concepts, aliases, references and relationships between different sources.
