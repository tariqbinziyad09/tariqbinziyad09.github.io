PROJECT OVERVIEW

From customer inquiry to solar proposal.

Solar AI Sales Agent combines an intelligent customer-facing sales assistant with an internal CRM for solar sales teams.

The AI assistant can collect customer requirements, understand appliance usage and monthly electricity information, estimate an appropriate solar system size and help convert an inquiry into a structured sales lead.

The CRM provides lead management, sales pipeline tracking, proposals, PDF quotation delivery, notes, follow-ups, tasks, staff management and role-based access.

The application is deployed as a complete web system with a React frontend, FastAPI backend and cloud PostgreSQL database.

BUSINESS PROBLEM

Why this system was built.

Solar sales teams often need to collect customer requirements, estimate system size, maintain lead records, prepare quotations and coordinate follow-ups across several separate processes. This project brings those activities into one AI-assisted sales workflow so customer conversations can move into structured CRM records and proposals.
